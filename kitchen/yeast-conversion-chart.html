The 1:1 Modern Swap vs. Traditional 25% Reduction

In everyday home baking, you can swap Instant Yeast and Active Dry Yeast 1:1 by volume (1 tsp for 1 tsp), knowing Instant Yeast will rise 15–20 minutes faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I store opened jars or bricks of yeast?

Store opened jars of Active Dry or Instant Yeast in an airtight container in the freezer for 2+ years of full rising power.

Why didn't my bread dough rise at all?

Either the liquid was above 138°F, the yeast was expired, or your kitchen is too cold.
