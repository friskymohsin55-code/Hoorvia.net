Frequently Asked Questions

How many cups of batter are in one standard boxed cake mix?

One standard 15.25 oz boxed cake mix yields approximately 4.5 to 5 cups of batter—enough for two 8-inch rounds, one 9x13 sheet, or 20–24 cupcakes.

When do I need a metal flower nail or heating core in a cake pan?

Any cake pan 10 inches or wider benefits from a greased metal flower nail placed upside-down in the center to conduct heat into the core.
