Air Fryer Fish & Seafood Cooking Times Cheat Sheet

Delicate fish proteins coagulate rapidly, meaning 90 extra seconds can turn buttery salmon or scallops into dry flakes. Most fresh seafood cooks in under 10 minutes with no flipping required when placed skin-side down on a perforated parchment liner. Target 145°F (63°C) for white fish and 130°F–135°F for medium-rare salmon.

How to Prevent Delicate Fish From Sticking to the Basket

White fish like cod, flounder, and tilapia have fragile muscle flakes that easily weld onto bare metal grates. Use these 3 foolproof strategies:

  1. No-Flip Skin-Down Rule: Always place skin-on fillets skin-side down.
  2. Citrus Sling or Parchment: Lay 3 thin lemon slices under your fillet, or use perforated air fryer parchment paper.
  3. Dry-Packed vs. Wet-Packed Scallops: Only buy 'Dry-Packed' scallops so they sear instead of steaming.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the white stuff oozing out of my air fryer salmon?

That is albumin, a natural protein pushed to the surface when fish heats too fast. Brining salmon in salt water for 10 minutes before cooking or pulling it at 135°F stops albumin from forming.

Can I put aluminum foil under fish in the air fryer?

Yes, as long as the foil sits purely on the inner basket grate, is weighted down by the fish, and leaves at least 1 inch of border open around the edges for air circulation.
