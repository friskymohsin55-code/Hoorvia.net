Rice Cooking Times & Liquid Ratios Master Reference

Not all rice cooks at the same speed. Specialty grains like Thai sticky rice require overnight soaking and steaming, while black forbidden rice takes nearly an hour. Use this master reference for simmering times across specialty rice varieties.

How to Steam Authentic Thai Sticky Rice Without a Special Steamer

Glutinous rice (sticky rice) cannot be boiled in a pot of water like regular rice or it dissolves into paste. Authentic preparation requires steeping and steaming:

  1. Soak 4 Hours Minimum: Cover sticky rice in cool water and let rest on the counter for 4 to 12 hours.
  2. Use a Cheesecloth or Fine Mesh Insert: Drain the soaked grains and place them inside a lined steamer basket set over boiling water. Cover with a tight lid and steam for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Instant Pot rice burning on the bottom with a 'Burn' error?

Starch settled at the bottom of the inner pot before pressure built up. Always add liquid first, pour rice in without stirring so it stays submerged, and make sure no loose grains touch the hot side walls.

Can I freeze cooked rice for meal prep?

Yes! Spread freshly cooked, steamed rice onto a baking sheet to cool rapidly for 15 minutes, then pack into freezer bags. Reheat in the microwave with 1 teaspoon of water added.
