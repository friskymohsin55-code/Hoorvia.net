Air Fryer Beef & Steak Cooking Times Cheat Sheet

Cooking beef in an air fryer delivers an edge-to-edge crust while keeping kitchen smoke minimal. Because steak timing depends heavily on cut thickness and starting meat temperature, always take steaks out of the fridge 20 minutes before cooking and preheat the basket at 400°F for 5 full minutes.

How to Get a Steakhouse Crust in an Air Fryer

Unlike a cast-iron skillet, an air fryer cooks primarily through hot air circulation. To achieve a deep brown Maillard crust on steaks or steak bites without overcooking the center:

  1. Dry Brine & Pat Dry: Surface moisture creates steam. Pat steaks thoroughly dry with paper towels and salt them 30 minutes prior.
  2. Max Preheat (5 Minutes at 400°F): Never place raw steak into a cold basket.
  3. Slice Against the Grain: For leaner cuts like flank, skirt, or sirloin roast, slice thin at a 45-degree angle perpendicular to the muscle fibers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my steak Bites turn out grey and boiled?

Your basket wasn't preheated or was overcrowded. Preheat empty at 400°F for 5 minutes, pat the meat cubes dry, and cook in a single spaced layer for just 5–7 minutes.

When should I add cheese to air fryer burgers?

Turn the air fryer OFF as soon as the burger hits 160°F, place the cheese slice on top, and close the drawer for 60 seconds. Leaving the fan running blows the cheese slice right off the patty!
