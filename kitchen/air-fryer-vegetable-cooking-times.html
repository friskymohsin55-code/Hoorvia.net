Air Fryer Vegetable Cooking Times & Prep Chart

Air frying caramelizes natural vegetable sugars twice as fast as oven roasting. However, vegetables split into two distinct density classes: dense starch/root vegetables (which need 400°F and 15–22 minutes) and tender high-moisture greens (which scorch if cooked past 8–10 minutes).

Why Broccoli Burns & Zucchini Turns Mushy (And How to Fix Both)

Every vegetable has a unique water-to-fiber ratio that dictates how it reacts in an air fryer:

  • The Broccoli & Brussels Sprout Trick: Pouring 1 tablespoon of water into the bottom drawer creates a micro-steam bath that softens the stems while the top fan crisps the florets.
  • The Zucchini & Eggplant Salt Rule: Toss zucchini in oil and pepper first, air fry at 390°F, and salt immediately after plating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I roast different vegetables together in the same basket?

Only if they are in the same density group! You can mix bell peppers, zucchini, and mushrooms (all 8–10 min), or carrots, potatoes, and squash (all 15–18 min).

How much oil do vegetables need in an air fryer?

Exactly 1 to 1.5 teaspoons per 4 cups of chopped vegetables is enough to coat the exterior and conduct heat evenly.
