Air Fryer Pork Cooking Times & Temperature Chart

Modern pork is much leaner than it used to be, meaning the old habit of cooking pork chops to 165°F makes them tough and dry. The USDA safe temperature for whole pork cuts is 145°F (63°C) followed by a 3-minute rest (only ground pork and sausages require 160°F/71°C).

The 145°F Rule for Pork Chops vs. 195°F for Ribs & Belly

Pork cuts fall into two opposite culinary categories that require different internal temperatures:

  • Lean Loin Cuts (Chops & Tenderloin): Very low collagen. Cook strictly to 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  • Tough Collagen Cuts (Ribs & Pork Belly): Safe at 145°F, but tough to chew until internal temperatures reach 190°F–200°F (88°C–93°C), where fat renders and connective tissue melts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep thin boneless pork chops from drying out?

Brine them in salted water for 15 minutes or coat them in 1 teaspoon of mayonnaise before seasoning. Mayonnaise is an oil-and-egg emulsion that locks moisture in and helps spices stick.

Should I pierce Italian sausages before air frying?

No! Piercing the casing lets all the flavorful fat leak out into the bottom drawer, leaving you with a dry, crumbly link. Cook at 375°F instead of 400°F so casings don't burst.
