Baking Troubleshooting Chart (Cakes, Cookies & Bread)

Baking is applied chemistry: every flopped cake, flat cookie puddle, or dense loaf leaves behind clear physical clues. Use this diagnostic matrix to match what went wrong to its scientific cause and fix.

■ CAKE, MUFFIN & BROWNIE TROUBLESHOOTING
Visible Baking FlawPrimary Chemical / Oven CauseHow to Prevent It Next Time
Cake Sinks in the Dead CenterToo much leavening, underbaked core, or oven door opened earlyMeasure baking powder level; bake to 200°F internal; don't peek before 25 min
High Cracked Volcano DomeOven too hot OR too much flour (crust sets before center rises)Spoon & level flour (120g/cup); lower temp -25°F; use wet cake strips
Dry, Crumbly & Coarse CrumbOverbaked by 5+ min OR not enough fat/sugarPull cakes right at 200°F–205°F; wrap cooled layers in plastic wrap
Tough, Rubbery & Tunnel-HoledOvermixed batter after adding flour (excess gluten developed)Fold dry ingredients in by hand or on lowest mixer speed just until combined
Sticks to Bottom of PanCooled too long in pan (sugars cement) OR un-parchmented bottomAlways trace a parchment circle for the bottom; invert after 10–12 min rest
Blueberries / Choc Chips SankThin batter OR wet heavy mix-insToss berries/chips in 1 tbsp dry flour from recipe before folding in
■ COOKIE TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX
Visible Cookie FlawPrimary Chemical / Oven CauseHow to Prevent It Next Time
Spread Into Flat Greasy PuddlesButter too warm/melted, hot baking sheet, or too little flourChill dough 1 hr; use cool parchment-lined pan; add 2 tbsp flour
Hard, Dry & Hockey-Puck StiffOverbaked in oven OR cups of flour were dipped & packedPull when center still looks pale & soft (9–11 min); weigh flour at 120g/cup
Didn't Spread at All (Thick Balls)Too much flour OR dough baked rock-hard straight from fridgeLet chilled dough balls sit 10 min on counter; flatten tops slightly
Burnt Bottoms, Pale TopsDark non-stick baking sheet OR baked on bottom oven rackSwitch to light shiny aluminum half-sheet pans on the middle rack
■ YEAST BREAD & PIE CRUST TROUBLESHOOTING
Bread / Pastry IssuePrimary Chemical / Oven CauseHow to Prevent It Next Time
Dense, Heavy Brick LoafDead yeast, water >138°F killed yeast, or under-kneaded glutenProof yeast at 105°F–110°F; knead until dough passes the 'windowpane test'
Gummy, Sticky Bread InteriorSliced while still hot OR underbaked below 195°FBake lean bread to 208°F; cool on wire rack a full 90 minutes before cutting
Mushroom Top Overhanging PanOver-proofed in pan (rose more than 1 inch above rim)Bake when dough rises 1 inch above rim and holds a gentle finger dent
Tough, Shrunken Pie CrustToo much water + overworked gluten + not rested before bakingKeep butter ice-cold; chill rolled crust in freezer 20 min before blind-baking
💡 Chef's Pro Tip: Baking Powder vs. Baking Soda Expiration Test: Drop 1/2 tsp Baking Powder into 1/4 cup warm water (should fizz). Drop 1/4 tsp Baking Soda into 2 tbsp vinegar (should erupt).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my cheesecake crack down the middle?

Bake in a water bath to 150°F, turn the oven OFF, crack the oven door 2 inches, and let it cool inside for 1 hour.

Can I salvage a cake that came out slightly dry?

Poke tiny holes across the top with a toothpick and brush generously with simple syrup.
