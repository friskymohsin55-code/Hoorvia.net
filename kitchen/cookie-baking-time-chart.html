Cookie Baking Time, Temperature & Scoop Size Chart

Cookies continue baking on the hot aluminum sheet pan for 3 to 5 minutes after leaving the oven. Pull cookies when the outer 1/4-inch rim is golden-brown and the center still looks pale, puffy, and slightly underdone.

Parchment Paper vs. Silicone Mats (Silpat) for Cookies

Parchment paper provides gentle surface friction so cookies grip the paper, resulting in thicker centers and crisp, golden bottoms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bake frozen cookie dough balls straight from the freezer?

Yes! Do not thaw them. Lower the oven temperature by 15°F (to 335°F) and add 2 to 3 extra minutes to the regular bake time.

How do I fix cookies that spread slightly out of round?

Immediately after pulling the hot tray from the oven, place a large round cutter or upside-down mug over each hot cookie and swirl it gently for 3 seconds.
