How to Soften Brick-Hard Brown Sugar in 30 Seconds

Place hardened brown sugar in a microwave-safe bowl, cover with a damp paper towel, and microwave in 15-second bursts until soft.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I swap powdered sugar for granulated sugar in cookies?

Only by weight (200g), not volume, because powdered sugar weighs 120g per cup and contains 3% cornstarch.

Can I reduce the sugar in a cake recipe by 50%?

You can safely cut sugar by 20% to 25% without losing texture, but 50% makes cakes dry and tough.
