Air Fryer Cooking Times Chart

Air fryer times and temperatures for 25 common foods, in Fahrenheit and Celsius, for chicken, vegetables, fries and snacks.

Air fryers cook fast with hot circulating air, but times vary by food size and thickness. This chart gives tested starting points for 25 common foods so you can stop guessing, avoid burnt edges, and get crisp results without overcooking. Always check the thickest part with a thermometer and shake the basket halfway through cooking for even browning.

How to Print & Use This Chart

Print this sheet on US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) in the USA and Canada, or on A4 in the UK. Choose “Save as PDF” from your browser print dialog if you want a PDF copy, then pick Letter or A4 in the paper-size setting — the sheet is designed to fit both without shrinking the text. For a kitchen that gets splashed, print on slightly heavier paper or laminate the sheet and stick it on the fridge with a magnet, inside a cupboard door, or next to the air fryer. Lamination also lets you wipe off oil and flour with a damp cloth, which is why many cooks keep this chart up for months.

To use it well, preheat for 3 minutes unless your model says otherwise, and never crowd the basket — crowded food steams instead of crisping. Cut pieces to a similar size so everything finishes together, shake or flip halfway, and treat every time as a starting point: a thick chicken breast needs longer than a thin one, and older air fryers run hotter or cooler. A cheap instant-read thermometer removes the guesswork for meat and fish. If food browns too fast, drop the temperature by 10–15°F and add a couple of minutes; if it stays pale, raise it slightly. Write your own model’s best times in the margin after a few cooks, and this generic chart becomes your personal chart.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do I need to preheat my air fryer?▼

Preheat for about 3 minutes for most foods. It helps crisping and keeps times closer to this chart, but very small snacks can go in cold with 1–2 extra minutes.

Why is my food not crispy?▼

Usually the basket is too full, pieces are wet, or there is no light oil spray. Dry food well, cook in a single layer, and shake halfway.

Are these times exact?▼

No — they are conservative starting points. Thickness, starting temperature (fresh vs frozen) and air fryer model all change times. Always check doneness, especially for meat.

Can I cook frozen and fresh food at the same time?▼

Avoid it. Frozen food needs more time and releases water, which makes fresh food soggy. Cook in separate batches.
