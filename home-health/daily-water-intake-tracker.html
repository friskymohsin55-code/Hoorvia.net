How to Print & Use This Chart

Print on US Letter or A4 and stick it on the fridge, beside your desk, or inside a planner — wherever you will actually see it during the day. Use “Save as PDF” in the print dialog for a PDF copy in Letter or A4. Laminate one copy and use a dry-erase marker if you want a reusable daily tracker, or print a week’s worth at once and keep them in a folder so a new day is always ready. The tracker works best when it is visible: a sheet buried in a drawer will not remind you to drink at 11 a.m. or 3 p.m., when most people fall behind.

Start the day with a glass, keep a bottle at your desk, and tick a box each time you finish a glass rather than trying to remember totals at night. Spread intake through the day — drinking a litre at 9 p.m. to “catch up” will disturb sleep and is not the same as steady hydration. Eat water-rich foods too: soups, fruit and vegetables all count toward fluid intake, even though this tracker counts glasses. You will need more in heat, during exercise, or when ill, and less if a clinician has advised fluid restriction for a medical condition — in that case, follow their guidance, not this general guide. If you have persistent thirst, very low urine output, dizziness, or a condition affecting kidneys, heart or blood sugar, discuss fluid intake with a clinician rather than pushing to a higher target on your own.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How much water do I really need?▼

A common starting guide is 30–35 ml per kg of body weight per day, adjusted for heat, exercise and health. Use the table as a guide, not a strict rule.

Does coffee or tea count?▼

They contribute fluid, though water is best. Do not rely on sugary drinks for hydration.

Is 8 glasses a day correct for everyone?▼

No — needs vary by size, activity and climate. Eight 250 ml glasses is a handy average for many adults, but check your weight guide above.

Should I drink a lot at night to catch up?▼

No. Spread drinks through the day. A large amount late at night disrupts sleep and is not a substitute for steady intake.
