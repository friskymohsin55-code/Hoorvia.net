Tire Pressure & Tread Chart: Printable Guide

Print our tire pressure and tread chart: recommended PSI and tread depth for 12 vehicles — cars, SUVs, trucks, motorcycles, bikes, and more.

Tire pressure is the cheapest safety check on a car: underinflated tires wear the edges, waste fuel, and overheat on the highway, while overinflated tires wear the middle and grip less in the rain. The right pressure is on the driver's door sticker, not on the tire — the number on the tire is the maximum, not the recommendation. Tread depth is the other half: the legal minimum is 2/32 of an inch, and winter tires need twice that to grip snow. The penny test works — if you can see Lincoln's head, the tread is too shallow. This printable chart lists twelve vehicles with recommended PSI, tread depth, and when to replace. Consult the live reference table below, or print the sheet and keep it in the glove box.
