Food Reheating Times Chart: Printable Safe Leftover Guide

Print our food reheating times chart: safe oven temps and times to reheat chicken, casseroles, pizza, rice, and more to a steamy 165°F.

Reheated food should be just as good as the day it was made — instead, most leftovers come back soggy, dried out, or lukewarm in the middle. The problem is usually the method, not the food: the microwave is fast but uneven, and room-temperature serving leaves bacteria perfectly happy. The USDA rule is simple and absolute — reheat all leftovers to 165°F, and bring sauces and soups to a rolling boil. This printable food reheating times chart gives you the right method, temperature, and time for twelve common leftovers, from crispy pizza to steamy rice. Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to reheat leftovers in the microwave?▼

Yes, if done right. Cover the food, stir or rotate halfway, and verify 165°F with a thermometer — microwaves heat unevenly and leave cold spots where bacteria survive. Let it stand 1–2 minutes after for even heat.

How many times can I reheat leftovers?▼

Once is the safe rule. Every cooling and reheating cycle passes the food through the danger zone again and ruins the texture. Portion out only what you will eat right now.

Can I eat leftovers cold?▼

Cold leftovers that were stored safely are fine for many foods — cold pizza and pasta salad are classics. But reheating to 165°F is the only way to be sure bacteria picked up during storage are destroyed.
