Metric to Imperial Conversion Table: Exact Formulas & Printable Reference

Close enough is not close enough when a conversion factor has to be exact. Rounding 2.20462 to 2.2 might slide for luggage, but it compounds in engineering, medicine, and trade — and nobody wants to discover the error in the finished product. Most online converters hide the math, which means you cannot reuse the factor, cannot check it, and cannot teach it. This printable metric-to-imperial conversion table shows the exact formula for every dimension instead: fourteen rows covering length, distance, weight, volume, temperature, area, and speed, each with the metric unit, its imperial equivalent, and the precise multiplier or equation to convert it yourself. Consult the live reference table below whenever you need the real factor — not the rounded guess — or print the sheet below and keep it where the numbers matter.

💡 Quick Tips

Go Metric to Imperial, Not the Other Way: These factors are tuned for metric-to-imperial; divide by the same factor to reverse the direction.

Keep Four Decimal Places: Rounding 2.20462 to 2.2 is fine for luggage, not for lab work — use the full factor for precision.

Temperature Needs a Formula: Only temperature uses an equation instead of multiplication — it is the one row worth memorizing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit exactly?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 (1.8), then add 32. So 25°C becomes 45 plus 32, or 77°F. To go back, subtract 32 first, then multiply by 5/9.

What is the exact factor for kilograms to pounds?

Multiply kilograms by 2.20462 — that is the exact internationally defined factor. Four decimal places are enough for anything outside a calibration lab.

How do I reverse a metric-to-imperial conversion?

Divide by the same factor instead of multiplying. Since 1 kg equals 2.20462 lb, one pound equals 1 divided by 2.20462 kg, or about 0.4536 kg.
