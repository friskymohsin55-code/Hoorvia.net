PEMDAS Order of Operations Chart: Printable Math Steps & Examples

Two students can solve 2 + 3 × 4 and get two different answers — 14 and 20 — and only one of them is right. The order of operations is the agreement that keeps math from being a matter of opinion: parentheses first, then exponents, then multiplication and division left to right, then addition and subtraction left to right. The classic traps are everywhere: dividing before multiplying because D comes after M in the acronym, or adding before subtracting when the subtraction sits first. Calculators follow the same rules, but they hide the work, so a wrong keystroke looks like a right answer. This printable chart lists the six PEMDAS steps with their symbols and six worked examples that catch the exact traps students fall into. Consult the live reference table below, or print the sheet and tape it to a desk or fridge.

💡 Quick Tips

M and D are equals: Multiplication and division share one step — work them left to right, never M before D.

A and S are equals too: Addition and subtraction share one step, so 10 − 3 + 2 is 9.

Say the steps out loud: Saying P-E-M-D-A-S before each problem stops the classic mistake: solving left to right.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does multiplication always come before division in PEMDAS?

No. Multiplication and division are the same step, worked left to right. In 8 ÷ 2 × 4 you divide first and get 16; doing the multiplication first gives 1, which is wrong. The acronym's order is just a memory aid.

What does PEMDAS stand for?

Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, Subtraction. It is also taught as BODMAS or BEDMAS in some countries, where B stands for brackets and O for orders — the rules are identical, only the letters differ.

Why do parentheses come first in the order of operations?

Parentheses group a calculation into a single value before anything else touches it. In (2 + 3) × 4 the parentheses force the addition to happen first, giving 20, while without them the multiplication wins and the answer is 14.
