Turkey Cooking Time & Thawing Chart: Printable Weight, Defrost & Roasting Guide

Roasting a centerpiece holiday turkey without stress comes down to backward scheduling: knowing exactly how many pounds to buy, which day to move the frozen bird into the fridge, and how many hours it needs in a 325°F oven. Misjudging thaw times by even a day can leave you with an icy cavity on Thanksgiving morning, while guessing oven times often dries out the breast meat before the dark thigh meat finishes cooking. This printable turkey cooking time and thawing chart puts the entire timeline on a single page for birds ranging from 8 to 24 pounds, plus bone-in turkey breasts. You get exact guest portion counts, refrigerator and cold-water defrost durations, and side-by-side roasting windows for both unstuffed and stuffed turkeys. Review the complete schedule below, or print the sheet below to pin on your refrigerator a week before your big meal.

💡 Quick Tips

Three-Point Thermometer Check: Always verify at least 165°F in three places—the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the center of the stuffing—without touching bone.

Foil Shield & Rest: Tent the breast loosely with foil once golden brown (~2 hours in), and rest the finished turkey for 30 minutes before carving.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pounds of turkey do I need per person?▼

Plan for 1.25 pounds of whole bone-in turkey per guest if you want generous portions plus leftovers for sandwiches the next day. For smaller appetites without leftovers, 1 pound per person is sufficient.

Why is it better to bake stuffing in a casserole dish instead of inside the turkey?▼

Stuffing packed inside a turkey cavity slows down heat transfer. By the time the center of the stuffing reaches the mandatory safe temperature of 165°F, the outer breast meat often overcooks past 175°F and turns dry.

How long can a thawed raw turkey stay in the refrigerator before cooking?▼

Once a frozen turkey has completely thawed in the refrigerator at 40°F or below, it remains safe in the fridge for an additional 1 to 2 days before you need to roast it.
