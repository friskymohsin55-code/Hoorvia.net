Steak Grill Times Chart: Printable Minutes-Per-Side Grilling Guide

Grilling a steak is a race between the seared crust forming on the outside and the center climbing to your target doneness. Because a half-inch skirt steak and a two-inch tomahawk behave like completely different foods over the same flames, timing by guesswork is how expensive cuts turn gray and disappointing. This printable steak grill times chart gives you exact minutes per side for ten popular cuts at high direct heat (450–500°F), plus the pull temperature that accounts for carryover cooking. Use the table below while your grill preheats, or print the sheet below to keep by the barbecue for consistently edge-to-edge perfect steaks.

💡 Quick Tips

Pull 5°F Early: Thick steaks keep cooking off the grill. Pull every steak 5°F below your target doneness and rest it — carryover heat finishes the job while juices redistribute.

Rest Half the Cook Time: Rest thin steaks 5 minutes and thick cuts 10 minutes, loosely tented with foil. Cutting too early spills the juices you just worked to keep inside.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I grill steak with the lid open or closed?▼

Sear with the lid open so moisture escapes and the crust browns deeply, then close the lid to finish thicker steaks with ambient heat. For cuts over 1.5 inches, use two-zone heat: sear over direct flames, finish over indirect.

How do I get crosshatch grill marks?▼

Sear 2 minutes, rotate the steak 45 degrees without flipping, sear 2 more minutes, then flip and repeat. The rotation creates the diamond pattern — it is purely visual, but it signals an even, confident cook.

Why is my grilled steak tough even at medium-rare?▼

Toughness at the right temperature usually means the wrong slice. Always cut against the grain — especially for flank, skirt, and hanger — because short muscle fibers chew tender regardless of the cooking method.
