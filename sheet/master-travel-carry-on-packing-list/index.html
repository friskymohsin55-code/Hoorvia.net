💡 Quick Tips

Roll, Don't Fold: Rolling clothes saves space and prevents deep creases. Pack socks and chargers inside shoes to use every inch of the bag.

Respect the 3-1-1 Rule: Liquids must be 3.4 oz or less, in one quart-size clear bag, one bag per passenger. Pull it out for the X-ray bin before your turn.

Pack for a Week, Not a Month: Plan outfits that mix and match, and do one mid-trip laundry. Seven days of clothes covers trips of any length.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard carry-on size limit?

Most airlines allow 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) including wheels and handles. Budget airlines are stricter, so measure your bag and check your airline's exact limit before packing.

Can I bring a power bank in my carry-on?

Yes — and you must. Spare lithium batteries and power banks are banned from checked luggage by the FAA and TSA. They belong in your carry-on, within the airline's watt-hour limit.

How many days can I pack for in just a carry-on?

Indefinitely, if you do laundry. Pack 5–7 days of mix-and-match clothes and wash halfway through. Carry-on-only trips of two to three weeks are completely normal with this routine.
