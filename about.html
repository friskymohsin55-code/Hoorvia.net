About Hoorvia.net

Hoorvia.net is a free printable charts and cheat sheets website for readers in the USA, UK and Canada. We publish one-page sheets you can print and use at home: kitchen cooking charts, crafting temperature guides, home and health trackers, and tech shortcut references.

Our goal is simple: no long stories, no email signup, and no paywall for a basic printable. Each post gives you the table itself on the page, a clean printable preview, and a Print / Save as PDF button that works on US Letter and A4 paper.

We aim for accurate, conservative values and we mark approximations as approximations. Where safety matters — for example meat temperatures — we follow published official guidance and cite it on the page. If you spot an error, please use the Contact page to tell us so we can correct it.
